Features

What RowPilot Can Do

Covering all features needed for both on-water and ergometer training. Empower both rowers and managers with a single app.

Home Screen

Check weather, tide times, rigging settings, monthly total distance, and recent sessions at a glance. Includes a built-in global search across all data in the app.

RowMode

High-precision stroke rate (SPM) automatic calculation via the built-in accelerometer and GPS tracking. The UI automatically optimizes based on device orientation.

PM5 Connectivity (Practice Tab)

Connect via Bluetooth to Concept2 rowing ergometers (PM5) to send and receive target workouts. Multi-erg synchronized training and batch transmission require the Pro plan (up to 4 ergs) or Manager plan and above (up to 10 ergs).

Rigging Management

Record boat and oar settings down to the millimeter. Precision SwiftUI vector diagrams dynamically adjust to your input values, visualizing your current setup intuitively.

Tide & Weather Forecasts

Based on Japan Meteorological Agency predictions, the app automatically identifies the nearest station from 239 locations across Japan via GPS to display tide graphs, alongside wind direction and speed critical for rowing.

Emergency SOS

Instantly send your current location (with a map link) to registered emergency contacts in case of a capsize or injury.

The Story Behind RowPilot

A severe shortage of GPS stroke rate meters in our school rowing club—that was where development began. Dedicated commercial units are expensive, making it hard to equip every boat. So why not use the smartphones everyone already carries? That spark launched roughly a year of development.

At first I envisioned a simple rate meter app, but requests like "I want to check tide times inside the app" and "I want to link ergometer data too" kept stacking up—until it grew into a unified app covering both water and land training.

"Communicating with the PM5 was genuinely tough. I hit a wall that days of troubleshooting with AI couldn't solve. On a long shot, I emailed Concept2 headquarters in English, and their engineer Ryan kindly helped me through it. Ultimately, I succeeded in sending workouts simultaneously to 9 ergs and maintaining two-way communication across 8 of them."
"Quite possibly a world first."

Part of the revenue goes toward donations to the Kochi Rowing Association. I continue developing RowPilot with the sincere hope that an app built by one high school student alongside AI can help elevate the rowing community, even in a small way.

Kaito Nakahira
RowPilot Developer
