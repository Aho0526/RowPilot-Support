Important Notice

Functional Restrictions & Auto-Cleanup
on Team Plan Migration

Starting from v1.0.1, system behavior when changing subscription plans after creating a team—such as downgrading or expiring without renewal—has been clearly defined.
A formal control flow has been introduced to prevent unauthorized continued usage and manage team capacity properly.

Effective Date: July 4, 2026

Implementation Date: July 20, 2026 (v1.0.1 (Build 2))

In RowPilot, when subscription details are modified on plans that include team features (Team, MAX, Organization), the following automatic synchronization, restriction, and cleanup flows apply. Required actions and grace periods vary depending on the target plan.

1. Migration to a Higher Team Plan (e.g., Team → MAX / Org)

When upgrading to a plan that supports more members, the plan change takes effect automatically as follows:

Immediate Sync

Automatic Quota Sync & Limit Expansion

Your team's subscription status synchronizes automatically in the background, immediately expanding your maximum member capacity to the limit of the new plan.

2. Migration to a Lower Team Plan (e.g., Org → MAX / Team)

When switching to a team plan with a smaller member limit, if your existing member count at the time of migration exceeds the new plan's limit, the following adjustment and auto-cleanup flow applies:

Day 1 – Day 30

1-Month (30-Day) Grace Period & Warning Display

A 30-day grace period begins as soon as the plan transition completes. During the grace period, a warning banner appears at the top of the team management screen, and a "Scheduled for Removal" warning badge is shown next to excess members who joined most recently.

* "Day 1" refers not to the date you requested the change, but to the date your current billing period ends and the transition to the new plan officially completes. Your previous member limit remains active until the end of the current billing period.

During Grace Period

Manual Adjustment by Administrator (Member Cleanup)

During the grace period, the team owner or administrator can manually remove inactive members to adjust the roster. Once the member count falls within the new limit, the warning banner and scheduled auto-removal are lifted automatically.

Upon Access After Day 30

Automatic Removal of Excess Members

If the member count is not brought within the limit by the deadline, excess members (starting from the most recently added accounts) are automatically removed from the team upon the next team access after Day 30.

3. Migration to a Non-Team Plan (e.g., Org → Pro / Free)

When switching to an individual plan without team creation (Pro) or the Free plan, or when a team subscription expires, the following data protection and archival measures apply:

Day 1 – Day 30

30-Day Archival Grace Period

Upon detecting the plan transition, a 30-day archival grace period is established. Throughout this period, all team features—including uploading new workout records—remain available without restriction.

* "Day 1" refers not to the date you initiated the change, but to the date your current team plan's billing period expires and the account actually switches to the new plan (or lapses). Full functionality of your previous plan continues until the billing period ends.

After Day 30

"Archived (Read-Only)" State Applied

Once the 30-day grace period concludes, the team transitions to an "Archived (Read-Only)" state.

The entire team is frozen, and all members (including the owner) switch to read-only access.

Restrictions While Archived:
• Uploading new workout records is blocked, and the message "This team is archived (inactive). Please check with your team administrator." is displayed.
• All administrative actions—including renaming the team, regenerating invite codes, approving join requests, and changing member roles—are locked (disabled).
• Team data is never deleted: Unlike previous behavior, neither the team nor its workout records are deleted after expiration, so past training records remain viewable at any time.

Restoring an Archived Team:
If you resubscribe to or upgrade back to a plan with team creation capabilities either during the 30-day grace period or after archiving, the archived state is lifted automatically and your team returns to normal operation.

Contact

If you have any questions regarding these specification changes or how your data is handled when switching plans, please contact our support desk at rowpilot.jp@gmail.com.
