Important Notice

Subscription Pricing Revision

We are significantly reducing monthly subscription prices across all plans to make RowPilot more accessible. The Organization plan will also be discontinued.

Price Change Effective Date: October 1, 2026 (automatic)

Organization Plan Discontinued: September 10, 2026 at 23:00 JST

Published: August 22, 2026  /  Last Updated: September 10, 2026

Thank you for using RowPilot. We are pleased to announce the following revision to our monthly subscription pricing.

Pricing Overview

Monthly subscription fees will be significantly reduced across all plans. New prices will take effect automatically on October 1, 2026. The Organization plan was discontinued on September 10, 2026 at 23:00 JST.

Plan Current Price New Price Change
Free ¥0 JPY / month ¥0 JPY / month No change
Pro ¥600 JPY / month ¥200 JPY / month ↓ ¥400 JPY off
Manager ¥900 JPY / month ¥500 JPY / month ↓ ¥400 JPY off
Team ¥3,000 JPY / month ¥1,000 JPY / month ↓ ¥2,000 JPY off
MAX ¥5,000 JPY / month ¥3,000 JPY / month ↓ ¥2,000 JPY off
Organization ¥8,000 JPY / month Discontinued Discontinued

Organization Plan Discontinuation

The Organization plan was discontinued on September 10, 2026 at 23:00 JST. Existing Organization plan subscribers will be automatically migrated to the MAX plan.

For current Organization plan subscribers:
Migration to the MAX plan is the primary transition path. Detailed migration guidance will be provided separately.

Background

RowPilot aims to be an app that athletes, coaches, and teams of all levels can use without financial barriers. This price reduction was implemented with the goal of lowering the cost of entry and contributing to the growth of the rowing community and the improvement of training environments.

We appreciate your continued support of RowPilot.

Contact

If you have any questions regarding this announcement, please feel free to contact our support team at rowpilot.jp@gmail.com.
