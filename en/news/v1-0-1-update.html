Version 1.0.1 Released

v1.0.1 Update Released & Pro Plan Feature Expansion

RowPilot v1.0.1 is now live on the App Store. Alongside major new features and refinements across measurement, PM5 sync, and team management, the Pro plan gains new capabilities at the unchanged ¥200/month price.

Published: November 5, 2026 / Released: November 16, 2026

Thank you for using RowPilot. Version v1.0.1 is now available on the App Store. Update your app to start using all of the new features and improvements detailed below.

1. New Features Added

Focused on multilingual support, PC browser connectivity, and team operations, the following capabilities have been added:

Multilingual Support (6 New Languages)

Added support for German, French, Spanish, Italian, Simplified Chinese, and Korean, bringing total supported languages to 8 alongside Japanese and English.

Team Web Dashboard Integration

Connect with the PC/tablet browser Team Web Portal to review team workouts, aggregate stats, and export CSV files. Supports custom team URL slugs, Web Coach invitations, and one-tap app login approval.

Team Workout Filtering & Batch Upload

Added keyword search (athlete name, notes, boat type) as well as workout type and date-range filters for team workouts. Also added batch uploading to share past unuploaded local records all at once.

RowMode Motion Sensitivity & Display Settings

Added a Motion Sensitivity slider (0.03 G to 0.30 G) inside RowMode display settings to fine-tune stroke detection thresholds, plus a toggle to display battery status in portrait orientation.

Nationwide Tide Map & Reference Water Level

Added an interactive map view to select pins and preview tide curves across 239 observation stations in Japan, along with a configurable reference water-level line on the tide chart.

Device Lock Reset & Guided Inquiry Form

Added admin tools to unlock device authentication for members switching phones, automatic approval checks for pending join requests, and a confirmation-backed inquiry form specifying the target screen and element.

2. Changes & Improvements

Measurement accuracy, Bluetooth reliability, team infrastructure, and data synchronization stability have been significantly improved:

Unified Cloudflare D1 Team Backend

Retired legacy CloudKit team management and unified everything onto Cloudflare D1. Streamlined advanced team settings, invite code regeneration, ownership transfer, team workout deletion, duplicate join-request prevention, offline retry queues, and plan/role edge-case handling.

PM5 Sync & Interval Progress Display

Optimized BLE command queue timeouts and status polling, and brought split CSAFE frames for Variable Intervals into full specification compliance. Fixed fixed-interval setup, blocked stale post-reset notifications, and added live per-interval remaining distance, remaining time, and completed interval counts.

RowMode Detection, GPS & End Flow

Refined dynamic axis selection and auto-start detection, decoupled real-time GPS accuracy display from distance filtering, renamed the finish button to "End", added save confirmation prompts even for sessions under 10 seconds, and removed post-session landscape lock.

Accurate Session Averages & Ghost Race Fixes

Saved workouts now record true whole-session averages (average 500m pace, average SPM, and average watts) rather than final instantaneous values across on-water RowMode, Practice, and Manager Mode. Also fixed Ghost Race start interpolation and finish detection.

Rigging 2D / 3D View & Presets

Refined the 3D rigging view and added selection between bow-mounted wing riggers and straight riggers, improved 2D/3D rendering positions and precision, and organized blade presets.

CSV Export, iCloud Sync & Subscription Sync

Ensured lazily loaded chart data, crew info, and GPS routes are reliably hydrated and saved during CSV export and iCloud sync. Integrated StoreKit 2 JWS verification, automatic subscription refresh on app foreground, revised pricing, and Organization plan deprecation.

3. Pro Plan Feature Expansion & Unchanged Price

Starting with v1.0.1, the following features have been added to the Pro plan at no extra cost, keeping the October 1 revised price unchanged at ¥200 / month.

Select Any of 239 Tide Stations Nationwide

In addition to auto-displaying the nearest station via GPS, freely pick any of the 239 JMA tide observation stations across Japan from the map or list to check tide times and curves in advance for camps and regattas.

Simultaneous Workout Sync for up to 4 PM5s

Connect up to 4 Concept2 PM5 rowing ergometers simultaneously and batch-transmit workout menus, enabling synchronized training for a four-person crew on the Pro plan alone.

Multi-Seat Crew Lineup Management

Alongside single sculls, create and manage multi-seat crew lineups from double sculls through eights.

4. Pro Plan Feature Summary After v1.0.1

Updating to v1.0.1 unlocks all of the following capabilities on the Pro plan:

  • All Free features (GPS measurement, real-time SPM display, unlimited session history, emergency SOS, etc.)
  • PM5 connection up to 4 machines (expanded from 1 machine in Free; workout send/receive)
  • ForceCurve graph display (stroke force curve visualization)
  • Ghost Race feature (real-time racing against past sessions)
  • Select & preview any tide observation station nationwide via map
  • Multi-seat crew lineup & management (Double through Eight)
