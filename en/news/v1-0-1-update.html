Upcoming v1.0.1 Update Details & Pro Plan Feature Expansion

Announcing the upcoming update details for RowPilot v1.0.1. Includes multilingual support, interface refinements, Pro plan price kept at ¥200 JPY/month, and new Pro features upon release.

Published: November 5, 2026

Thank you for using RowPilot. We are pleased to share the details of our upcoming version v1.0.1. These updates will become available as soon as v1.0.1 is released and updated on your device.

Key Updates in v1.0.1

The following enhancements and improvements are included in v1.0.1:

Multilingual Support

Added support for 6 new languages: German, French, Spanish, Italian, Simplified Chinese, and Korean, allowing rowers and teams worldwide to use RowPilot in their native languages.

Rigging 3D View Fine-Tuning

Refined the 3D vector diagram rendering and touch controls in the rigging settings screen for smoother, millimeter-accurate visualization.

Tide Screen Refinements

Improved layout and precision of tide forecast curves and water information, making pre-practice condition checks even clearer.

Team Management Edge Case Handling

Handled previously unhandled edge cases in team features, greatly improving member management and permission stability.

Pro Plan Feature Expansion & Price Kept Unchanged

Starting with v1.0.1, new features will be added to the Pro plan. The price remains at ¥200 JPY / month at no additional cost.

Choose and Preview Tide Observation Points Nationwide

In addition to auto-detecting the nearest station via GPS, you will be able to select and view tide curves from 239 observation stations nationwide.

PM5 Workout Synchronization for up to 4 Machines

Pro users can connect up to 4 Concept2 PM5 rowing ergometers simultaneously via Bluetooth and batch-send workout menus for synchronized crew training.

Pro Plan Feature Summary (from v1.0.1)

All features included in RowPilot Pro after updating to v1.0.1:

  • All Free features (GPS rate meter, SPM real-time display, unlimited session history, two-way communication with 1 PM5, emergency SOS)
  • ForceCurve graph display (visualize stroke force curve)
  • Choose and preview tide observation points nationwide
  • PM5 connection up to 4 machines (workout sync)
  • Crew management

Contact Support

For questions or feedback regarding this update, please contact support at rowpilot.jp@gmail.com.
