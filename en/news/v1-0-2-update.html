v1.0.2 Update Notice, Apology & Refund Information Regarding the 1-Month Free Trial

Alongside the release details for RowPilot v1.0.2, we sincerely apologize that the 1-month free trial did not take effect across any paid plan, and provide refund instructions for eligible subscribers.

Published: November 21, 2026

Thank you for using RowPilot. In this notice, we share our sincere apology and refund procedure regarding the 1-month free trial issue, together with the new features and improvements introduced in v1.0.2.

1. Apology & Refund Information Regarding the 1-Month Free Trial

Although the in-app subscription screen displayed "First Month Free" and "1-Month Free Trial", we discovered that the 1-month free trial did not take effect for any of the paid plans: Pro, Manager, Team, and MAX. We deeply apologize for the inconvenience and concern this has caused.

In v1.0.2, we updated the subscription screen to check StoreKit 2 introductory offer eligibility dynamically so that free trial labels are shown only when a free trial offer is configured and actually applicable.

Eligible Subscribers Users who subscribed to the Manager plan on November 20, 2026
Refund Method Bank Transfer or PayPay Remittance
How to Request Your Refund
If you subscribed to the Manager plan on November 20, 2026, please contact our support desk at rowpilot.jp@gmail.com with the following details. We will process your refund promptly after verification.
  • A screenshot of your Apple receipt email or purchase record showing the subscription date and time
  • Your preferred refund method: Bank Transfer or PayPay
  • Destination details: bank name, branch name, account type, account number, and account holder name for Bank Transfer, or your PayPay ID or registered phone number for PayPay

2. New Features Added

Added direct BLE heart rate sensor pairing, heart rate and calorie tracking with charts, and in-app server account deletion.

Direct BLE Heart Rate Sensor Pairing Across All Modes

Added a dedicated screen to connect standard BLE heart rate sensors directly to iPhone with auto-reconnect and battery status display. Together with automatic reading from PM5-paired heart rate straps, live heart rate tracking works across on-water RowMode, Practice, and Manager Mode.

Heart Rate & Calorie Recording and Graph Visualization

Workouts now save average heart rate, maximum heart rate, and calories across local record details, team workout details, and CSV exports. Added a Heart Rate tab to the workout graph screen and improved chart rendering accuracy.

In-App Server Account Deletion

Added a server account deletion option in Settings. You can permanently delete your server profile, team memberships, and uploaded team workout records from inside the app while keeping local workout records on your device intact.

3. Changes & Improvements

Strengthened PM5 communication reliability, spec-compliant workout validation, team workout pagination, data integrity, security, and sensor stability.

PM5 Reliability, Spec-Compliant Input Limits & Disconnect Protection

Added a sequential CSAFE transmission queue in Practice Mode, tightened standard frame byte-stuffing, and stabilized NFC connection flows. Aligned workout duration, split, interval, calorie, and 50-interval limits with Concept2 PM5 specifications, and added a prompt to save progress if PM5 disconnects mid-workout.

Manager Mode Dashboard & Race View Refinements

Added a Save button and variable interval progress display to the Manager dashboard, plus Back and Save buttons, lock-state integration, elapsed-time tie-breaking, and rotation handling in Race View. Updated the PM5 sample-rate selector to a segmented 1 Hz / 2 Hz / 4 Hz control.

Team Workout Pagination, Permissions & Navigation

Added 500-record pagination in the team workout list and hardened decoding against malformed records. Exposed the team member list and Web login approval actions to regular members, removed duplicate settings entries, shortened pending approval polling to 30 seconds, and restricted Manager plan sharing strictly to the Manager role.

Offline Sync, iCloud Restore & Record Integrity

Moved pending team upload queues from UserDefaults to compact file storage, prevented external imports from being auto-uploaded, and introduced deletion tombstones so deleted workouts do not reappear from iCloud. Hardened multi-device Keychain auth sync, edit cancellation, and committed-data sharing.

Subscription Eligibility & Security Hardening

Added dynamic introductory-offer eligibility checks, revocation and refund status syncing to the server, serialized auth verification, stricter Apple JWS x5c certificate chain validation, and CSV formula-injection escaping. Completed cleanup of legacy Organization plan definitions and separated personal plan status display.

RowMode, SOS Alarm, Tide & Weather Stability

Moved the active SOS alarm stop banner to a global overlay visible across all tabs and activated the audio session only while the alarm is active. Refined RowMode background auto-start standby and pause-cancel behavior, locked tide calculations to JST with instant refresh on nearest-station change, and improved weather request handling.

Contact

For refund requests or any questions and feedback regarding this update, please contact our support desk at rowpilot.jp@gmail.com.
