Bluetooth & Connection
I cannot connect to a Concept2 PM5 via Bluetooth ▼

Please try the following steps in order:

  1. Select "Connect" from the PM5 monitor menu to make Bluetooth communication available.
  2. Open iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Bluetooth and confirm that RowPilot is granted Bluetooth permission.
  3. Start a Bluetooth scan in the Practice tab, and your PM5 will appear in the device list.
  4. If the connection is unstable, temporarily turn off or move away from other nearby Bluetooth devices.
  5. Verify that your PM5 firmware is up to date (tested and verified on firmware version v178.037 and later).

If the issue is still not resolved, please contact us by email.

How do I connect multiple ergs simultaneously? ▼

Full multi-erg monitoring (up to 10 machines) is available to individual Manager plan subscribers, or to users granted the "Manager" role by an admin on a Team or MAX plan.
Note that the Free plan supports 1-on-1 PM5 connection, and the Pro plan supports connecting up to 4 PM5 machines.

Enable Bluetooth on each PM5 individually, then open RowPilot's Practice tab → Manager Mode and start scanning to view nearby PM5 monitors.

Tap each machine you want to include in order to connect.

Data & Backup
Where is my data stored? ▼

RowPilot data is automatically saved to your local device storage and your personal iCloud account. If team synchronization is enabled on a Team plan or above, your team's numerical workout statistics are also stored on an external server (Cloudflare D1).

How do I transfer my data when switching devices? ▼

RowPilot primarily uses local device storage, so if you uninstall the app while iCloud sync is turned OFF, your local data will be deleted. However, as long as iCloud sync remains turned ON, signing in with the same Apple ID on your new iPhone allows you to carry over your data seamlessly.

Cross-platform data migration between iPhone and Android is not currently supported.

We plan to release cross-platform transfer support alongside the upcoming Android release.

I want to delete my recorded data ▼

Individual workout records can be deleted by swiping on them in the in-app History screen.

If you want to erase all workout records completely, go to in-app Settings → "Delete All Workouts".

(To delete team data uploaded to the sync server, select the desired workout stored inside your team view and delete it.)

Features & Usage
How do I set up and use the SOS feature? ▼

Setup: Go to the "Settings" tab → "SOS Contact Settings" and pre-register the recipient's phone number and the user's name.

Usage: Tapping the SOS button on RowMode (the GPS stroke rate meter screen) immediately prepares a message containing your current GPS location for your registered contact. We strongly recommend configuring this before heading out on the water.

Note: SOS messaging uses the iPhone's built-in Messages app (SMS). Location permission must be granted to RowPilot to include GPS coordinates.


⚠️ Disclaimer: The SOS feature is strictly an auxiliary aid and does not replace official emergency services (police, fire, or coast guard). We assume no liability whatsoever for any damages arising from activation delays, non-operation, or undelivered messages. Please review Article 8, Paragraph 1 of the Terms of Use for full details.

I want to change the tide observation station ▼

On the Free plan, RowPilot automatically retrieves and displays tide data from the nearest Japan Meteorological Agency observation station based on your current GPS location.

If you wish to freely select and preview any observation station nationwide—such as for training camps or regatta venues—please upgrade to the Pro plan.

Stroke detection in RowMode is not responding well ▼

Please try testing with another stroke rate meter app once. If strokes still cannot be detected, there may be an issue with your device's accelerometer, or the rowing stroke acceleration may be very light.

If detection remains unresponsive, please reach out to us directly.

Team Management & Team Web
How do I log in to Team Web (PC / browser dashboard)? ▼

Teams on the Team or MAX plan can browse and aggregate workout records from a PC, tablet, or Android browser via the Team Web Portal. There are two ways to log in:

1. Web Coach Email Login (for head coaches & staff without the iOS app):
When a team owner or admin registers an email address in the iPhone RowPilot app under "Team Settings → Web Dashboard → Manage Web Coaches", an invitation link is sent to set an initial password. After setting the password, coaches can log in with their email address and password.

2. One-Tap Approval in the RowPilot App (for rowers & managers with the iOS app):
Use the button, QR code, or 6-digit verification code displayed on Team Web and approve it under "Web Login Approval" inside the iPhone app to sign in without a password.

What is a team custom URL (slug)? ▼

Team owners or admins can configure an easy-to-remember custom URL in the format rowpilot.jp/teams/your-slug/ from "Team Settings → Web Dashboard" inside the iPhone app.

Entering the identifier on the Team Web Portal or opening the custom URL directly will take you straight to your team dashboard when signed in.

After switching phones, I see "Device Authentication Lock" and cannot access my team ▼

To prevent unauthorized access using the same user ID, team accounts are bound to your device.

If a device lock occurs after switching phones or reinstalling the app, ask your team's owner or admin to tap "Unlock Device Authentication" for your member entry in Member Management, then try again.

Can I batch-share past workouts recorded before joining a team? ▼

Yes. Starting in v1.0.1, you can use the "Batch Upload Records" feature on the in-app Team screen to upload unshared workouts stored on your device to your team all at once.

Subscription
What happens to team data if I change or cancel (downgrade) a team plan? ▼

To prevent unauthorized use and manage server resources properly, certain functional restrictions and automatic cleanup rules apply when changing plans.

If your team exceeds the member limit of the new plan, a 30-day grace period applies before excess members are automatically removed. If you switch to a plan without team features (Pro/Free) or your subscription expires, the team enters an "Archived (Read-Only)" state after a 30-day grace period (past team data is preserved and never deleted).

For full details on restrictions and how to lift them, please see the announcement below:

Auto-Cleanup & Functional Restrictions on Team Plan Changes (Details) →

How do I cancel a Premium plan? ▼

On your iPhone, go to Settings → your Apple ID at the top → Subscriptions → RowPilot → Cancel Subscription.

If you cancel before your next billing date, you can continue using the plan through the end of the current billing period. Canceling never deletes your workout data.

How do I restore my purchase? (After changing devices or reinstalling) ▼

Open the app and tap Settings → RowPilot Premium → Restore. As long as you are signed in with the same Apple ID, your active subscription will be recognized automatically.

If restoration fails, check iPhone Settings → App Store to make sure you are signed in with the Apple ID used for the purchase.

Can I share my subscription via Family Sharing? ▼

App Store Family Sharing is not supported. To use RowPilot across a team, please use the Manager role sharing feature included in the Team and MAX plans—one coach or advisor can subscribe and grant Manager Mode access to team managers.

Other
How do proceeds reach the Kochi Rowing Association? ▼

Proceeds remaining after App Store commission fees (15% *), taxes, and minimal server maintenance costs are donated once a year by the developer to the Kochi Rowing Association. Donations help maintain rowing equipment and support junior and youth rowers.

* Because we participate in the Apple Small Business Program, the App Store commission rate is reduced from the standard 30% to 15%.

Where can I view the open-source code? ▼

The source code is published on GitHub: github.com/Aho0526/RowPilot

It is made public to ensure transparency in data handling and app behavior. All copyrights remain with the developer, and unauthorized secondary use is prohibited.
