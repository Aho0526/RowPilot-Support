2. Purpose of the App

The App is intended to assist in recording, analyzing, and reviewing rowing and related training sessions. It makes no guarantees regarding competitive performance, health, or safety.

3. Safety

The App includes features designed for use on the water. When operating the App on the water, pay close attention to surrounding conditions and your own safety, and immediately discontinue use whenever any hazard may arise.

We assume no liability whatsoever for lost or damaged devices, collisions, or any other accidents occurring in connection with on-water use of the App. Please use the App strictly at your own risk.

4. Information Collected

The App may obtain the following information:

  • Location data (GPS): to record and display workout distance, speed, pace, and route
  • Time of day and elapsed duration
  • Device information (such as OS type, for operational verification)

Collected information is strictly limited to what is necessary to provide the App's features. Except when using the Team Management feature (Section 6.1), all information is processed locally within the App and is never transmitted externally.

5. Handling of Location Data

The App uses location data (GPS) for the purpose of recording workout distance, speed, and route. Location data is acquired only when permitted by the user in their device settings.

Acquired location data is used solely within the scope of providing the App's features; we never view, collect, or disclose such location data to third parties. Users may revoke location access at any time via their device settings.

6. Data Storage

Workout records are stored locally on the user's device. Although data may be lost when switching devices or deleting the App, backing up via iCloud allows records to be retained. We assume no responsibility for any loss or corruption of data.

6.1 Team Management Feature

For clubs and organizations, the RowPilot Team plan and above provide functionality to store workout records collectively across a set number of members. Records of users belonging to a team are automatically transmitted to a Cloudflare D1 database server and shared within the team.

Data transmitted and stored during this synchronization includes:

  • User-configured display name
  • Session summary
  • Workout metrics and records

Regardless of whether a session is recorded indoors (via PM5 connection) or outdoors (on the water), only numerical workout statistics and summaries are synchronized; location data such as GPS coordinates and movement routes is never transmitted to or stored on the server.

6.2 Data Storage via iCloud

Personal workout records—both indoor and outdoor—can optionally be backed up to the user's own iCloud account. Because these backups include on-water workout records, location data is included in the backup; however, the data is stored in encrypted form, and neither the developer nor Apple can access its contents.

8.1 SOS Feature

The App includes an SOS feature intended to assist in emergencies on the water.
Note: This feature cannot be used unless a user name and emergency contact phone number have been registered in Settings.

Pressing and holding the on-screen SOS button for 1.5 seconds and tapping the SMS send button sends an SMS containing your location coordinates, name, battery level, and timestamp to your registered contact. Please test and verify this feature prior to relying on it during practice.

The SOS feature is strictly an auxiliary tool and does not replace official emergency services such as police, fire, or coast guard dispatch. We assume no liability whatsoever for any damages caused by activation delays, non-operation, or undelivered notifications.

8.2 Use in Official Races

Because the App runs on a smartphone, bringing it aboard during official regattas may violate competition rules prohibiting communication devices in the boat. For official races, please use a dedicated stroke rate meter compliant with competition regulations.
