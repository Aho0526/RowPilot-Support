Privacy Policy

RowPilot is built around keeping data handling simple and transparent. This page explains the permissions requested by the app, the types of data handled, where data is stored, and our policies regarding third-party sharing.

1. Developer Access to Data

RowPilot is fundamentally designed around on-device processing. Your training data and settings are managed on your device or in your own personal cloud storage.

Training records, location data, settings, and emergency contacts are kept locally on your device by default. Except when using the team synchronization feature, data is never automatically transmitted to servers viewable by the developer.

RowPilot's source code is publicly available as open source on GitHub to ensure implementation transparency.

2. Permissions Requested and Their Purpose

RowPilot requests the following permissions from iOS. Each permission is used strictly for the purposes stated below.

Location Services (GPS)

Used for calculating boat speed and recording routes in RowMode, and for composing emergency messages in the SOS feature. GPS coordinates are collected only during active workout sessions and are never tracked continuously in the background.

  • Collection timing: Only during active RowMode workout sessions
  • Storage location: On-device
  • When SOS is triggered: Sent directly via SMS to pre-registered emergency contacts
Bluetooth

Used for wireless connectivity with Concept2 PM5 rowing ergometers. Communication takes place entirely between your iPhone and the PM5 device.

  • Connected devices: Concept2 PM5 only
  • Retrieved data: Training metrics such as distance, time, stroke rate, and pace
Motion & Fitness Sensors

Used for stroke rate detection in RowMode. Sensor data is used solely for real-time measurement processing.

  • Collection timing: Only during active RowMode workout sessions
Cloud Synchronization

When a team is created on a Team plan or above and a joined member enables team synchronization, numerical team workout metrics are stored on an external server managed by the developer (Cloudflare D1). Please refer to Section 5 for full details on stored data.

  • Applicable users: Users belonging to a team on the Team or MAX plan
  • Stored content: Numerical training statistics and in-app user IDs
SOS Contacts

Emergency contact details manually registered by the user (phone number and user name) are stored locally on the device. SOS messages are sent via the Messages app (SMS/MMS).

3. Data Storage Locations and Management

Data handled by RowPilot is stored and managed as follows:

Data Type Storage Location Developer Access
Training records (on-water) On-device / Personal iCloud None
Training records (ergometer) On-device / Personal iCloud None
GPS routes & coordinates On-device / Personal iCloud None
SOS contacts On-device None
App settings On-device / Personal iCloud None
Team management data (Team & above) Cloudflare D1 (Stats only) Stats only*

* Only numerical workout statistics (distance, time, stroke rate, etc.) are stored on the team synchronization server. GPS coordinates and movement routes are never included. Please see Section 5 for details.

4. Data Sharing Between Users

RowPilot includes a feature for sharing training data directly between users. Sharing is performed via Apple's AirDrop (peer-to-peer direct communication between devices). Because no intermediary server is involved, no extra data is stored on any third-party server.

AirDrop transfers are protected by Apple's end-to-end encryption, and recipients explicitly approve every incoming transfer.

5. Server Synchronization for Team Plans

On the Team and MAX plans, an external server managed by the developer (Cloudflare D1) is used to synchronize data across team members.

Data recorded on the server:

  • Numerical workout metrics (distance, time, stroke rate, pace, power, etc.)
  • Team member identifiers (in-app user ID and display name)
  • Only when using the Web Coach invitation feature: the login email address and authentication hash of Web Coaches explicitly registered by a team administrator

Data NOT recorded on the server:

  • GPS coordinates and movement routes (location data from on-water sessions)
  • SOS contacts
  • Personal identifiers of general members, such as Apple IDs or email addresses

6. Purchase and Billing Processing

RowPilot Premium subscriptions are processed through Apple's StoreKit 2.

  • Payment details (such as credit card numbers) are protected and managed exclusively by Apple and are never shared with the developer
  • The developer receives only confirmation of whether a subscription transaction succeeded
  • Subscriptions can be managed or canceled from your App Store subscription settings
  • For billing inquiries or refund requests regarding in-app purchases, please contact Apple Support

7. Third-Party Services

Third-party services currently used by RowPilot are as follows:

Japan Meteorological Agency Tide Data API

Retrieves publicly published tide prediction data from the Japan Meteorological Agency to render tide charts in the Tide tab. No personal user information is included in these API requests.

Cloudflare, Inc. (Cloudflare D1)

Uses Cloudflare D1 (serverless database) for team data synchronization on the Team and MAX plans. Please see Section 5 for details on synchronized data.

Strava Integration (In Preparation)

We plan to introduce workout synchronization with Strava in a future update. This feature will require explicit user authorization (OAuth) and will never share data without user consent. This policy will be updated when the integration launches.

RowPilot does not use advertising SDKs or behavioral analytics libraries (such as Google Analytics or Firebase Analytics). We do not track or profile user behavior.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as new features are added to RowPilot or in response to legal and regulatory changes.

  • When updated, the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page will be revised
  • For changes that materially affect how user data is handled, we will provide notice inside the app or on the support page
  • Continued use of the app after any revision constitutes acceptance of the updated Privacy Policy

9. Governing Law and Jurisdiction

This Privacy Policy is governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of Japan. Any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Policy shall be submitted to the exclusive jurisdiction of the court of first instance having jurisdiction over the developer's place of residence.
